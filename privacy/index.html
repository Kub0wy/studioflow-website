Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Introduction

StudioFlow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how StudioFlow Enable handles information when you use the plugin.

Information we collect

StudioFlow Enable does not collect, store, process, transmit or sell personal information.

The plugin does not require:

  • a user account,
  • registration,
  • login credentials,
  • analytics,
  • telemetry,
  • cloud storage,
  • advertising identifiers,
  • payment information.

Local project data

StudioFlow Enable operates locally within Adobe Premiere Pro.

The plugin may access the active Premiere Pro project and timeline only to perform actions requested by the user, such as enabling or disabling supported timeline items.

Project information and media files are not uploaded or transmitted to StudioFlow or any third party.

External links

The StudioFlow Enable header contains a link to the StudioFlow website. The website opens only after the user intentionally clicks the header.

Once the StudioFlow website is opened, the hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and server logs.

Third-party services

StudioFlow Enable runs inside Adobe Premiere Pro and depends on Adobe UXP APIs.

Use of Adobe products is also governed by Adobe’s own terms and privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

StudioFlow Enable is intended for professional creative software users and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when StudioFlow products or website functionality change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
