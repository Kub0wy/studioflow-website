# Welcome to StudioFlow --- Why I Started Building These Tools

If you've landed here, chances are you work in post-production too.

For more than 15 years I've been working as a video editor on
documentaries, TV shows, commercials, and all kinds of productions with
impossible deadlines, constantly changing edits, and thousands of media
files.

Over the years I noticed something interesting.

The biggest problems in editing are rarely creative.

They're usually the tiny, repetitive tasks that interrupt your flow
dozens of times every day. Clicking the same buttons. Hunting for a
one-frame mistake. Preparing deliveries. Organizing projects. Waiting
for files to copy while wondering if everything actually transferred
correctly.

None of these problems are particularly exciting.

But solving them is.

That idea eventually became **StudioFlow**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Built by an editor, for editors

StudioFlow grew naturally from everyday work in the edit suite.

Almost every tool started the same way.

I found myself repeating the same task over and over again and thought:

> "There has to be a better way."

So I started building small utilities that solved real problems I was
facing inside Adobe Premiere Pro.

The goal has never been to reinvent editing.

It's to remove friction from it.

If a plugin saves a few minutes every day or prevents a single mistake
before delivery, it's already doing its job.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What exists today

### StudioFlow Enable

Enable was born from one very specific workflow.

When preparing an **AAF** for the sound department, I often need to
deliver **every audio clip**, even though during editing I've
temporarily disabled many of them to keep the timeline clean.

Premiere Pro doesn't offer a simple **Enable Everything** command.

If you select multiple clips and use the built-in Enable option,
Premiere simply **toggles** their state. Enabled clips become disabled,
disabled clips become enabled, leaving you with another mixed selection
instead of everything enabled.

So I built a tool that simply does what I expect.

One click.

Everything enabled.

Or everything disabled.

It's an incredibly small plugin, but after using it for a while, going
back feels surprisingly frustrating.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### StudioFlow FrameGap

One-frame gaps.

Two-frame gaps.

Three-frame mistakes hiding somewhere in a long timeline.

Every editor knows how annoying they can be.

FrameGap scans your sequence and immediately points you to those tiny
problems before they become someone else's problem.

It's one of those tools you hope never finds anything.

But you're always happy when it does.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### StudioFlow Delivery

Another workflow that kept repeating itself involved preparing projects
for other departments.

Typically I would duplicate my edit several times, remove unnecessary
tracks, lock the right ones, prepare one version for the colorist and
another one for audio post-production, export everything, double-check
it, and hope I hadn't forgotten a single step.

That gets old very quickly.

The idea behind StudioFlow Delivery is simple:

Configure everything once.

Save your presets.

Click one button.

Then go grab a coffee while the plugin prepares your deliveries exactly
the way you like them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### StudioFlow Project Manager

This project is currently under active development.

The goal isn't to replace Premiere's built-in Project Manager.

Instead, I want to make large productions easier to organize, navigate
and maintain, especially when projects grow over months of editing and
involve multiple editors.

There's still plenty to build, but it's already becoming one of the
projects I'm most excited about.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What's coming next

StudioFlow is growing beyond Premiere Pro plugins.

Several larger desktop applications are already in development, each
focused on solving a different part of the post-production workflow.

### StudioFlow QC

A quality control application designed to analyze video and audio files
before delivery.

The goal is simple:

Catch technical problems before your client, broadcaster or streaming
platform does.

### StudioFlow DataMaster

Probably the most ambitious project in the StudioFlow family.

DataMaster is a completely new copying and verification engine designed
for media professionals moving huge amounts of footage every day.

The focus is reliability, speed, intelligent verification and confidence
that every copied file is exactly where it should be.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## This is a passion project

People often ask how much work goes into building software like this.

The truth is that it rarely feels like work.

I genuinely enjoy discovering those tiny frustrations that every editor
quietly accepts as "that's just how Premiere works" and trying to build
a better solution.

Creating StudioFlow is my way of sharing the tools I wish I had years
ago.

Some of them save only a few clicks.

Some save several minutes every day.

Some may save an entire delivery.

And honestly, that's what makes building them so much fun.
