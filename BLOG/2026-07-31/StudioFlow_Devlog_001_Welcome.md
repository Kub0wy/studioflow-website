## Why I built it

Like many StudioFlow tools, Enable wasn't created because I wanted to add another plugin to the marketplace.

It came from a small frustration I kept running into almost every day.

When I'm preparing an AAF for the sound department, I often need to include every audio clip in the timeline. During editing, however, I frequently disable clips that I don't want to hear at the moment.

The problem is that Premiere Pro doesn't have a true Enable All command.

If you select everything and choose Enable, Premiere simply toggles the current state. Clips that are already enabled become disabled, while disabled clips become enabled.

Instead of ending up with everything enabled, you get another mixed selection.

It sounds like a tiny issue.

Until you have to do it every single day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Another everyday workflow


I also often use temporary **Adjustment Layers** for quick color corrections while editing.

They're incredibly useful during the creative process, but before sending a project for finishing or exporting references, I often want to disable every temporary color adjustment with a single click.

Again, Premiere doesn't provide a quick way to do exactly that.

Enable does.
